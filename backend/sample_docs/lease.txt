RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Section 1: Term
This lease begins on January 1, 2026 and ends on December 31, 2026.

Section 2: Rent
Tenant agrees to pay $1,800 per month, due on the 1st of each month.
A late fee of $75 applies if rent is not received within 5 days of the due date.

Section 3: Termination
Either party may terminate this lease by providing 60 days written notice.
Early termination by the tenant before the lease end date requires payment
of an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

Section 4: Security Deposit
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,800, refundable within 21 days
of move-out, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

Section 5: Pets
Pets are allowed with a non-refundable pet deposit of $300 per pet.
No more than two pets are permitted without written landlord approval.
